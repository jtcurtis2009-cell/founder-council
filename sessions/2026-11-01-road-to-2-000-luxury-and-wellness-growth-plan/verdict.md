# Founder Council verdict: Road to 2,000 (luxury and wellness)

**Session:** 2026-11-01-road-to-2-000-luxury-and-wellness-growth-plan · Plan mode · 5 seats, 3 rounds

## Decision
How does NOVIQ get from **236 units** to **2,000 units** (about 1,200 STR units and 800 hotel keys under management contracts) while moving into luxury and wellness real estate? What target date and guardrails should it set?

**Verdict: A leads (8.1), with C close behind (8.0). Run both from day one. B and a gated D fill the STR gap. Target: 2,000 units by Q4 2030, with gates that allow a slip to Q4 2031.**

## Context and assumptions
- About $10M a year, treated as **gross bookings** (about $42k per unit), so fee revenue is about **$1.5–2M** at 20% and 15% fees. If $10M is NOVIQ's own revenue, the company has roughly 5x more capacity to fund this plan, and the guardrails still apply.
- Team of 40, only 2 in sales. Recently acquired a 21-unit PM company. The main channel is owner switchers.
- Manages 3 hotel buildings for third-party owners, with no leasing or owning. *Assumption:* about 170 STR units and about 66 hotel keys today.
- *Assumptions:* about $1–2M of growth cash and no outside equity. Owner churn is not measured, so the plan assumes 10–15% a year. The revenue-increase guarantee is uncapped today.
- San Diego has 829 Tier 3 licenses left and Oceanside's coastal cap is 480, so STR growth in the core market comes mostly from switchers.
- A hotel key earns about $3.7k a year in fees, against about $8–10k for an STR unit (industry.md, [E]).
- The wellness real estate market size ($1T by 2030 / $1.8T by 2035) comes from the founder and has not been verified. Check it with the Global Wellness Institute before using it externally.

**Rough check.** Gross STR adds needed are about 1,300 (about 1,030 net, plus about 280 to replace churn at 10%). That is about 500 from A, 450 from B and 300 from D. Hotel keys need about 735 more, or about 10–12 contracts of 60–80 keys over 4 years.

## Option-by-option analysis
- **A.** *For:* It earns the most per unit, NOVIQ already runs it, and every audit adds data that helps win the next owner. *Against:* The license caps hold it near 500 units, and scaling it also scales the guarantee liability.
- **B.** *For:* The 21-unit deal proved it. It adds units in blocks and can buy luxury and desert books outside the license squeeze. *Against:* Acquired owners churn (Vacasa's lesson), and the cash spent on deals competes with leadership hires.
- **C.** *For:* No license cap and capital-light. One 80-key contract equals about 80 switcher closes, and the 3 current buildings are the proof. *Against:* Low fee per key, long sales cycles, and owners revolt when returns fall short (Life House).
- **D.** *For:* A wellness-led desert market plus MTRs gets around the coastal caps and fits the positioning. *Against:* A new market without density is how Selina and Vacasa broke. It stays gated until about Q4 2029.

## Guardrails (conditions)
1. **Guarantee:** Before hiring any new rep, make it a conditional floor paid as a fee rebate. Cap it at **one quarter's fees per unit**, settle it annually, keep total payouts at **10% of annual fee revenue or less**, and hold a funded reserve.
2. **Stay capital-light:** No master leases, key money, personal guarantees or callable debt. Hotels only on management contracts (about 3% base plus an incentive on GOP). Keep **6 months of fixed costs** in cash.
3. **Churn stop signal:** Measure net units and owner churn monthly. If annualized churn is above 10% for two quarters, pause acquisitions and the new market.
4. **Acquisitions:** Pay **2x fee revenue or less**, in cash or seller financing, with 30–50% of the price in earn-outs tied to 12–24-month retention. Run one integration at a time.

## Biggest risks and how to de-risk them
- **The guarantee grows into a liability.** Use the one-quarter cap, the 10% portfolio cap and a funded reserve, and stress-test it quarterly.
- **The founder is the bottleneck, with only 2 sellers.** Record the close as a playbook, hire a sales admin and manager before reps 4–6, and hire a hotel leader so C never runs through Justin.
- **Churn outruns adds.** Track net units monthly, use retention earn-outs, keep local density and pause expansion when churn passes 10%.
